# FLLM Broker Listing Page v1

**Status:** LOCKED / OFFICIAL  
**Approved:** September 17, 2026  
**Canonical live reference:** `/brokers/list-your-license`

This page is the approved FLLM broker-listing landing and submission experience.

## Locked page role

The page must continue to communicate that FLLM is an additional marketing channel for independent Florida liquor-license brokers without replacing the broker, taking over the client relationship, or sharing in the broker's commission for advertising-only listings.

## Locked hero structure

The hero must preserve:

- Official FLLM header/menu treatment.
- "Independent Broker Marketplace" positioning.
- Primary headline: broker client liquor-license listing on FLLM.
- Broker-control value proposition.
- Two clearly separated accepted listing structures:
  - Quota Liquor License Only
  - Quota Liquor License + Business Package
- Standard and Featured listing pricing:
  - Standard: $14.95 one time
  - Featured: $24.95 one time
- Featured positioning that includes 30-day priority exposure and listing-specific SEO work by FLLM, with no search-placement guarantee.
- Primary and secondary CTAs.

## Locked broker value messaging

The page must continue to communicate:

- Increased listing exposure and buyer traffic.
- FLLM augments the broker's existing marketing and listings on other sites.
- Broker remains the identified representative and transaction contact.
- Buyer inquiries route to the broker's designated contact.
- FLLM does not take a share of the broker's commission for advertising-only marketplace listings.
- License-only and business + liquor-license package listings are supported.
- Business-package listings may distinguish liquor-license asking price from total business-and-license package price.

## Locked resource coverage

The broker resources section should continue to provide access to:

- FLLM Transaction Services
- County market pages
- County maps and statewide heat maps
- FLLM Exchange Board / market activity
- Market reports and transaction evidence
- DBPR / ABT resources
- FDOR resources
- Florida liquor-license attorney directory
- Valuation / appraisal resources
- Financing resources
- ABT forms and transfer guides

## Locked behavior

- Shared official FLLM header/footer behavior remains authoritative.
- FAQ groups use the sitewide FLLM auto-open-on-hover / close-on-leave controller.
- Existing Standard / Featured selection and submission flows remain functional.
- Responsive behavior must be preserved.
- Do not replace the page with a new visual system or reintroduce long dense marketing paragraphs without explicit approval.

## Change control

Future edits should preserve the approved hierarchy, broker-facing positioning, navy / white / gold visual language, pricing, business-package capability, Featured SEO disclosure, resource-center structure, and broker-control messaging unless the user explicitly approves a new page version.
