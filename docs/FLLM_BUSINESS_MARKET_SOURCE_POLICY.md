# FLLM Business Market Source Policy

Effective: September 30, 2026

## Purpose

This policy governs new FLLM Market Listings for operating businesses that include or use liquor licenses.

## Discovery sources

Third-party marketplaces such as BizBuySell, BizQuest, LoopNet, BusinessesForSale.com, and similar listing websites may be used to discover potential opportunities. They are not sufficient, by themselves, to make a new business opportunity publishable on FLLM.

## Publication requirement

A new Market Listing may be published only when its material facts are supported by at least one source independent of the discovery marketplace, or when the broker or seller directly authorizes FLLM to publish the opportunity.

Preferred verification sources include:

- the broker's own website or brokerage listing page;
- the seller's own public page;
- Florida DBPR / ABT public records for license identity and status;
- county, property, corporate, or other public records where relevant;
- another independently published source that supports the particular fact.

DBPR verification of a license does not by itself verify that a business is for sale, its asking price, financial performance, included assets, or marketing authority.

## Content rules

- Write an original FLLM headline and summary.
- Do not copy third-party marketplace headlines or descriptive prose.
- Do not reuse third-party photographs, graphics, branding, or page layout without permission.
- Keep the actual business location and license classification accurate.
- Do not imply that a marketplace, broker, or seller sponsors or approves FLLM unless that relationship is documented.

## Discovery-only status

If a material fact cannot be independently verified, the candidate must remain discovery-only / review-required and must not appear in public Market Listing inventory, category pages, detail pages, or the sitemap.

## Legacy records

Market Listings created before this policy cutoff may remain as legacy records for progressive review. New Market Listings after the cutoff must be marked independently verified or broker-authorized before public publication.
