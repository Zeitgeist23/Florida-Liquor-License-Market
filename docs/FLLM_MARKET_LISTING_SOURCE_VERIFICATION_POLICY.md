# FLLM Market Listing Source & Verification Policy

Effective: September 30, 2026

## Purpose

Florida Liquor License Market (FLLM) may use public business marketplaces such as BizBuySell, BizQuest, LoopNet and similar services to discover potential Florida business opportunities that appear to involve alcoholic-beverage licenses.

Those marketplace pages are discovery sources only. FLLM should not treat their advertisements as the publishing source for a new FLLM Market Listing.

## Publishing rule for new Market Listings

A new third-party Market Listing may be published only when at least one of the following is true:

1. **Independent verification** — material listing facts are confirmed through an independent source, such as:
   - the broker's own website or brokerage listing page;
   - the seller's own public page;
   - Florida DBPR / Division of Alcoholic Beverages and Tobacco public records;
   - relevant property, corporate, tax, or other public records;
   - another independently maintained source that supports the material facts.

2. **Broker/seller authorization** — the broker or seller has directly authorized FLLM to publish the opportunity.

If neither condition is satisfied, the opportunity must remain **Discovery Only / Review Required** and must not be exposed as published FLLM Market Listing inventory.

## Content rules

For Market Listings sourced through third-party discovery:

- Do not copy the third-party advertisement headline.
- Do not reproduce descriptive prose from the marketplace advertisement.
- Do not reuse marketplace photographs, graphics, logos, branding, page layout, or screenshots without permission.
- Write an original FLLM title from factual fields such as county, business category, cuisine, license type, and price.
- Write original FLLM explanatory copy and market context.
- Keep the actual city/county and license classification accurate; do not relabel a nearby listing as being located in a target city for SEO.
- Do not identify a broker as Featured or imply a broker/FLLM relationship unless that relationship is documented.
- Treat asking price, revenue, cash flow, square footage, seating, license type and similar details as requiring source verification before publication.

## Discovery workflow

1. Find the opportunity on a public marketplace or other discovery source.
2. De-duplicate it against FLLM's existing inventory.
3. Locate an independent verification source.
4. Confirm the material facts that FLLM intends to publish.
5. Create an original FLLM market title and summary.
6. Publish only after the independent-source or direct-authorization requirement is met.
7. If independent verification is unavailable, retain the lead as Discovery Only / Review Required.

## Existing inventory

Market Listings published before this policy are treated as legacy inventory pending gradual source review. The policy guard in the FLLM codebase prevents newly numbered Market Listings from becoming public unless they satisfy the new source-verification rule.

This is an internal operating standard and is not legal advice.
