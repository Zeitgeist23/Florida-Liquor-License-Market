# FLLM Standalone License Buyer-Lead Referral Standard\n\n**Status:** LOCKED / APPROVED\n**Version:** v1\n**Approved:** October 1, 2026\n\n## Purpose\n\nThis standard governs FLLM's referral of a prospective buyer to a broker or license seller after FLLM receives a standalone liquor-license buyer inquiry.\n\n## Trigger\n\nA referral email may be prepared only after FLLM independently confirms, by phone call or email exchange with the broker/license seller, that matching standalone inventory is currently available in the buyer's requested county and license type.\n\nRequired match:\n- county\n- license type\n\nRequired internal verification:\n- broker/license seller email\n- availability confirmed = yes\n- confirmation method = phone or email\n- date/time of confirmation\n\n## Proprietary lead rule\n\nThe buyer inquiry belongs to FLLM's lead-intelligence workflow. The referral email must describe the buyer only as a county + license-type buyer.\n\nNever disclose:\n- the specific FLLM listing reference the buyer originally clicked or selected\n- the source marketplace or source advertisement\n- the source broker's asking price\n- language implying the buyer specifically requested the recipient broker's particular license\n\nThis preserves FLLM's ability to match the buyer to another available license if the initially observed inventory is no longer available.\n\n## Compensation rule\n\nThe first referral email must not mention commissions, referral fees, lead fees, compensation, commission sharing, or future compensation arrangements.\n\n## Required email structure\n\nSubject: `FLLM Buyer Lead — [County] [License Type] License`\n\n1. Florida Liquor License Market / Buyer Lead Notification\n2. State that FLLM received a prospective-buyer inquiry for the county + license type.\n3. State that FLLM confirmed the recipient currently has matching inventory available.\n4. Provide standardized Buyer Contact Information:\n   - Name\n   - Email\n   - Phone\n   - Preferred County\n   - License Interest\n5. Ask the broker/license seller to contact the buyer directly regarding available matching inventory, current pricing, availability and transaction terms.\n6. State that FLLM provides targeted marketplace exposure and buyer-lead generation for Florida liquor-license inventory.\n7. Include the low-pressure Featured Broker Listing invitation:\n   `https://www.floridaliquorlicensemarket.com/brokers/list-your-license`\n8. Use the official FLLM Client Services signature.\n\n## Canonical wording\n\nFlorida Liquor License Market recently received an inquiry from a prospective buyer seeking to purchase a **[County] [License Type] liquor license**.\n\nFLLM has confirmed that you currently have [County] [License Type] inventory available and is providing the buyer's contact information below for direct follow-up.\n\n**Buyer Contact Information**\n\n**Name:** [Buyer Name]\n**Email:** [Buyer Email]\n**Phone:** [Buyer Phone]\n**Preferred County:** [County]\n**License Interest:** [License Type]\n\nPlease contact the buyer directly regarding your available [County] [License Type] license inventory, current pricing, availability, and transaction terms.\n\nFLLM provides targeted marketplace exposure and buyer-lead generation for Florida liquor-license inventory. Brokers and license sellers can also add available inventory to FLLM to reach buyers specifically searching for Florida liquor licenses.\n\nIf you would like to add additional Florida liquor-license inventory to FLLM, you can review the Featured Broker Listing options here:\n\n`https://www.floridaliquorlicensemarket.com/brokers/list-your-license`\n\n## Anthony Genovese precedent\n\nThe October 1, 2026 St. Lucie County 4COP Quota referral to Anthony Genovese is the approved precedent for this standard. FLLM confirmed his matching inventory by phone before providing the buyer's contact information, and the email did not disclose that the buyer had originally submitted a specific-license inquiry.\n\n## Change control\n\nDo not change the proprietary lead rule, the confirmation prerequisite, or the no-compensation-language rule without explicit user approval.
