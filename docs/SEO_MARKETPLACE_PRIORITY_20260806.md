# FLLM marketplace-first SEO priority

FLLM's primary organic-search destination is the marketplace inventory at `/listings` and the 67 county market pages. Financing remains a secondary service page that may rank independently, but county pages and marketplace pages should primarily direct users toward current liquor-license inventory.

Search Console basis (7-day snapshot supplied August 6, 2026):
- `/financing`: 458 impressions, 0 clicks
- `/listings`: 378 impressions, 1 click
- `/counties/duval`: 163 impressions, 1 click

Implementation priorities:
1. Keep financing page metadata relevant to financing searches without promoting financing across the marketplace.
2. Strengthen `/listings` metadata, visible inventory language, structured ItemList data, and county-market navigation.
3. Make county pages feed buyers into filtered marketplace listings.
4. Remove dedicated county financing sections unless future Search Console/conversion data specifically justifies them.
